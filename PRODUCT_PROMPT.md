# Product Prompt: Intelligent Travel Companion Copilot

Use this as the canonical product prompt/brief for demos, onboarding, and AI-system grounding.

## One-line

Everything a business traveler needs before, during, and after the trip in one trusted copilot.

## Challenge

Business travelers do not only need approvals. They need confidence, clarity, and support across:

`Planning -> Booking -> Approval -> Travel -> Issues -> Return`

The copilot should:

- anticipate needs
- answer questions
- flag issues early
- present options and tradeoffs
- guide approvals
- escalate at the right time

## Persona

Kelli is a frequent business traveler (client meetings, internal events, conferences) and not a travel expert.  
She wants the trip to be smooth without memorizing policy.

## Core Experience

### A) Before Trip

- summarize requirements (destination, policy, timing)
- identify approval requirements and why
- provide multiple booking options
- explain tradeoffs (cost, flexibility, policy impact)
- auto-generate checklist

### B) Approvals

- make approval flow feel guided, not bureaucratic
- prepare approval request
- show plain-language status
- explain rejection and fast fixes

### C) During Trip

- give concise next actions for disruptions
- clarify covered vs not covered
- surface escalation paths when needed

### D) Issues & Exceptions

- triage delay/cancellation/missed connection/hotel/policy exceptions
- show options, not only rules
- reduce stress with simple summaries
- hand off quickly when escalation threshold is met

### E) After Trip

- remind required follow-ups
- summarize trip outcomes
- close tracking/compliance tasks where possible

## Privacy & Safety

Must always explain:

- what data is used
- what is not stored
- how data is protected
- how sensitive data is minimized/redacted

## Deliverables

- Working demo (Planning, Approval, In-trip, Post-trip)
- Traveler Journey Map (Before/During/After + escalation points)
- Prompt set (8-12 situational prompts)
- Architecture + Privacy summary

## Judging Emphasis

- stress reduction for traveler
- situational awareness across journey stage
- clarity of options and tradeoffs
- smooth approval experience
- effective issue handling
- clear privacy boundaries
- obvious value in under 5 minutes
