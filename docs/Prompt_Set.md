# Prompt Set (HackKU Demo)

1. What do I need for my trip to London next week?
2. Based on my saved trip, generate a pre-trip checklist and top risks.
3. Do I need approval for this option, and who needs to sign off?
4. This was rejected. What is the fastest fix to get approved?
5. Compare a cheaper nonrefundable fare vs a flexible fare for this trip.
6. My flight was canceled. Give me the best next 3 actions right now.
7. I missed my connection and have a client meeting tomorrow morning. What should I do?
8. Is this hotel issue something I can handle myself or should I escalate?
9. Open an escalation case for this disruption and tell me what to include.
10. What is still open after my trip, and what should I complete today?
11. Generate post-trip follow-up tasks and mark expense-report as done.
12. For this answer, label what came from app context vs web search.

## Expected Response Traits

- concise, low-stress language
- explicit options/tradeoffs
- policy-safe framing with enterprise guardrails
- one concrete next step
- escalation boundary clarity when disruption risk is high
